## Allowed roles for AI

- literature search and query expansion
- structured extraction
- hypothesis generation

- anomaly review
- critique and alternative explanations
- experiment suggestions

## AI is not final authority for

- arithmetic or statistics
- source provenance
- raw evidence
- execution results
- physical measurements

Consequential AI-generated claims must be checked against primary evidence or deterministic computation.
