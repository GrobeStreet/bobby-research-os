# Research Constitution

Bobby Research OS exists to maximize trustworthy information produced per researcher.

1. Evidence outranks narrative.
2. Reproduction outranks novelty.
3. Falsification outranks confirmation.
4. External scoring outranks self-scoring.
5. Primary sources outrank summaries.
6. Simple baselines precede complex models.
7. Post-hoc analysis is labeled post-hoc.
8. Failed experiments remain in the record.
9. AI-generated claims require independent verification.
10. Every important result must trace back to code, data, and evidence.
11. Uncertainty is reported, not hidden.
12. Surprising results receive more scrutiny, not less.
13. Claims must not be stronger than their evidence level.
14. A project that dies honestly is a successful use of the system.
15. The repository is part of the scientific result.

## Operating loop

**Question -> Evidence -> Hypothesis -> Adversarial Tests -> Computation -> Falsification -> Replication -> Artifact**

AI may propose, search, summarize, scaffold, critique, and generate candidate tests. Deterministic systems, primary evidence, and independent checks verify consequential claims.
